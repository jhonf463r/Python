## Corrections required before canonicalization

Several statements in the supplied result are too strong or technically over-specified and must not be promoted unchanged:

1. `learning ⇒ ΔW` is too restrictive. Continual learning can change external memory, symbolic structures, routing, policies or other state without changing neural weights. The scientific criterion should be persistent post-experience capability/knowledge change with retention/generalization and controls, not a mandatory weight delta.

2. `knowledge revision ⇒ ΔR` is too restrictive. Revision can occur through belief status/provenance/priority/context changes without graph-topology change. Structural reorganization should be a separate hypothesis, not the definition of revision.

3. `metacognition ⇒ ΔD + ΔC` with mandatory pre-action adjustment is too narrow. Scientific metacognition includes monitoring/self-evaluation and control; control can be pre-action, concurrent or post-error depending on task. The discriminating feature is whether self-evaluative information causally informs monitoring/control, not merely whether an action changed before observation.

4. The result's biological analogies such as `weight adaptation ↔ synaptic plasticity` are too categorical. They should be classified as functional analogies unless a specific computational/biological mapping is justified. Avoid declaring digital mechanisms biologically equivalent merely because both alter connection strength.

5. The listed ADD/UPDATE/DOWNGRADE/etc. operations are a useful engineering ontology, but not all are standard AGM operations. AGM centers expansion, contraction and revision; update is a related but distinct belief-change paradigm. Do not present the whole IABV operation list as if it were directly established by AGM.

6. Several source attributions are incomplete (`Fiete et al.`, Lamme, Friston, etc.) and the response often says 'sources' without tying individual claims to exact primary/review references. The research therefore requires independent source-level audit before adoption.

7. Consciousness-indicator language must remain probabilistic/theory-relative. Current work explicitly describes indicators as credence-shifting and warns that no individual indicator or combination should automatically be treated as sufficient for consciousness.
