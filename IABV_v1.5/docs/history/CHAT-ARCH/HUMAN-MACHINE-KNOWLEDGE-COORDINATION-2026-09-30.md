# IABV v1.5 — HUMAN-MACHINE KNOWLEDGE COORDINATION
# Shared field for human intent, AI experience, evidence and traceability

## PURPOSE

The IABV shared knowledge field must preserve and organize the complete developmental interaction between:

- human objective, intuition, hypotheses, deductions, corrections and design intent;
- IABV's existing architecture and state;
- participating AI reasoning, experiments, critiques and observations;
- verification, provenance and causal evidence;
- resulting Knowledge / Relation / Routing / Method deltas.

The purpose is to strengthen **human-machine mutual understanding** without collapsing human intent, AI interpretation and verified system truth into one undifferentiated record.

## CORE LOOP

`HUMAN INTENT / OBJECTIVE
→ IABV FRAME ENTRY
→ RELEVANT KNOWLEDGE ACTIVATION
→ AI INTERPRETATION / ACTION
→ OBSERVATION
→ INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION
→ RECONCILIATION
→ KNOWLEDGE DELTA
→ HUMAN-INTENT / MODEL DELTA
→ TRACEABLE WRITEBACK
→ NEXT ITERATION`

The next participant should inherit the organized state of the collaboration, not merely the previous transcript.

## HUMAN CONTRIBUTION MUST REMAIN FIRST-CLASS

The field should preserve, when materially relevant:

- original objective;
- intuition that generated an architectural hypothesis;
- distinctions the human establishes;
- corrections to AI interpretation;
- rejected interpretations;
- desired developmental direction;
- decisions and governance boundaries;
- conceptual connections discovered across apparently separate topics.

Human ideas may be hypotheses rather than verified facts. Preserve their epistemic status.

## AI CONTRIBUTION MUST REMAIN FIRST-CLASS

For each material AI contribution preserve:

- activated knowledge;
- interpretation;
- capability used;
- action or experiment;
- observation;
- verification;
- correction;
- new invariant;
- changed routing or method;
- unresolved uncertainty.

The reusable unit is the transformation of the shared model, not the identity or authority of the AI.

## TRACEABILITY MUST CONNECT THE WHOLE CHAIN

For material changes preserve:

`HUMAN IDEA
→ AI INTERPRETATION
→ EXISTING IABV STRUCTURES ACTIVATED
→ ACTION / EXPERIMENT
→ ARTIFACT
→ BRANCH / REF / SHA
→ RUNTIME PROVENANCE
→ OBSERVATION
→ INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION
→ RECONCILIATION
→ KNOWLEDGE / RELATION / ROUTING / METHOD DELTA
→ HUMAN INTERPRETATION / DECISION
→ WRITEBACK`

Do not collapse:
- human hypothesis into system fact;
- AI report into evidence;
- repository evidence into runtime proof;
- runtime proof into causal effect;
- agreement into verification.

## ORGANIZATION OVER ITERATIONS

The field should progressively convert conversation into structured development:

`intuition → hypothesis → experiment → evidence → reconciled knowledge → relation → reusable method → future decision`.

Ideas that remain unverified should stay visible as hypotheses or unresolved knowledge rather than being silently promoted or discarded.

Repeatedly confirmed ideas may gain activation relevance, but confirmation must remain evidence-based and context-aware.

Repeatedly falsified ideas must become retrievable negative knowledge and should lose activation for contexts where they are invalid.

## HUMAN-MACHINE UNDERSTANDING

The desired outcome is not for the machine to replace human intent or for the human to manually maintain the machine's complete internal state.

Instead:

`human supplies purpose / direction / high-level judgment`
`IABV supplies structured continuity / evidence / organization`
`AI participants supply specialized reasoning / execution / challenge`
`verification supplies epistemic control`
`writeback supplies continuity`

The resulting field becomes a shared externalized working memory and traceability substrate until IABV can perform the same functions directly at runtime.

## CURRENT LIMIT

This protocol does not prove that IABV or any AI possesses consciousness, subjective experience or generalized shared understanding.

It defines an operational method for preserving and improving **mutual human-machine understanding** through explicit objective, provenance, evidence, experience and model-change records.
