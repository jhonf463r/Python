# 0. OBJECTIVE

Determine the **current real state** of IABV v1.5 regarding its ability to:

1. observe its own current state and developmental deficits;
2. derive a required capability from a real objective;
3. discover available external AI resources;
4. choose a capability-fit actor/resource rather than inheriting a hard-coded actor;
5. construct the canonical context/prompt for that actor;
6. actually dispatch work to **Devin** through the legitimate production path;
7. receive/capture Devin's response without manual transport where the production path claims to support automatic capture;
8. verify that the response belongs to the intended task/thread/execution;
9. ingest the verified experience back into IABV;
10. persist the resulting knowledge/decision evidence;
11. use that new evidence to alter a subsequent decision or next developmental action;
12. establish the smallest credible closed loop for **IABV-assisted own development**.

The audit must determine whether this loop is merely **defined**, **wired**, **invoked**, **observed**, **verified**, or **causally effective**.
