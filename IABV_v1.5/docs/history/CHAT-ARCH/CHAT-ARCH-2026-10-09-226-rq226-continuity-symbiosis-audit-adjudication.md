## ADJUDICATION

### Finding 1 — Entry-sequence inconsistency

Accepted: `CONFLICTING_SEQUENCE_OR_AUTHORITY` at the level of procedural order, with no evidence supplied that this order discrepancy already caused a specific wrong decision.

The audited source contains distinct orders for the same memory entry surfaces:
- README's earlier entry-order section;
- README's later single-routing-spine amendment;
- AI Frame Entry Protocol;
- Memory Operating Protocol.

The authority boundaries themselves are coherent: `CURRENT-STATE.md` governs current routing; the UAAL Constitution governs conceptual lineage; Context Index navigates; Memory Operating Protocol defines retrieval and epistemic method. The order of entry should be unified without merging those responsibilities.

### Finding 2 — UAAL activation trigger

Accepted: `PRESERVED_BUT_ACTIVATION_AMBIGUOUS`.

The root concept `UAAL-ROOT-001` and its machine-readable lineage exist. The entry protocol does not state with sufficient precision when a task must explicitly activate the conceptual root versus merely maintain a parent-concept link. A narrow trigger is appropriate: explicitly activate the root and relevant lineage when an objective touches global vision, architecture, universal semantics, cross-domain composition, learning, memory/continuity or protocol organization. Do not read the entire archive or Constitution for every narrow task.

### Finding 3 — Chat/transcript intake completeness contract

Accepted: `KNOWLEDGE_ACQUISITION_GAP`, bounded to a uniform operational contract for arbitrary human-supplied conversations.

Existing protocols preserve human intent and distinguish interpretation, evidence, verification and deltas. They do not establish one uniform intake record that reliably distinguishes a complete source artifact from an excerpt, segmented transcript or partially inaccessible material.

The intake contract must capture provenance, declared extent, actual accessible extent, inspected coverage, omissions/unknown segments, available byte-level identity when genuinely measurable, and an explicit completeness status. It must prohibit describing an artifact as completely read or absorbed when only part was available or inspected.

### Finding 4 — Application and causal reuse

Accepted: `APPLICATION_OR_REUSE_GAP` as an open verification frontier, not as proof that every new AI failed.

The report correctly distinguishes a written protocol from operational reliability. The existence of memory, retrieval rules or prior lessons does not prove that a new participant consistently retrieves the right material, changes its task constraints accordingly, or causally improves a later outcome. Preserve the distinction between persistence, retrieval, activation, decision impact and causal reuse. A later controlled/independent continuity test is required before stronger reliability claims.

### Finding 5 — No new memory organ justified

Accepted: the existing division of responsibility is sufficient for the proposed documentary repair. No new memory store, central brain, router or coordinator is warranted by this audit. Harmonize the existing entry and intake protocols and update only their owning projections.

## CHAT / TRANSCRIPT INTAKE CONTRACT

For each material supplied conversation, preserve at minimum:
- source identifier/name and source provenance (pasted text, attached artifact, Library item, URL or other origin);
- the source extent as declared and the extent actually accessible;
- segments/pages/lines/bytes actually inspected where measurable;
- coverage summary and known omitted, inaccessible or uninspected portions;
- byte-level hash only when the actual bytes were available and the digest was actually calculated; otherwise mark it unknown;
- completeness class: `FULL_WITHIN_DECLARED_ARTIFACT`, `PARTIAL_EXTRACT`, `SEGMENTED_INCOMPLETE` or `UNVERIFIABLE`;
- original human intent/idea/correction, separated from AI interpretation;
- claims classified as fact/report, inference, hypothesis, decision, verified result or unresolved;
- error/false-positive/contradiction/negative knowledge and the concrete future constraint it changes;
- Knowledge, Relation, Method and Routing deltas, owner layer, provenance links and next open edge.

Do not require a cryptographic digest where source bytes are not available. Do not claim complete reading, complete absorption or independent verification beyond the actual coverage. Preserve original-source identity separately from canonical synthesis identity.
