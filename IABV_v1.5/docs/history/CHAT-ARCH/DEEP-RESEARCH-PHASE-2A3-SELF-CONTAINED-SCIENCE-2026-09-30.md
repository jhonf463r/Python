# 5. SCIENTIFIC DOMAINS

## A. CONTINUAL / LIFELONG LEARNING AND PLASTICITY
Investigate continual/lifelong learning, online adaptation, stability-plasticity, catastrophic forgetting, replay/consolidation, transfer, generalization, compositionality and evidence separating temporary adaptation from durable reusable learning.

## B. KNOWLEDGE / BELIEF REVISION
Investigate AGM belief revision, truth-maintenance systems, non-monotonic reasoning, belief retraction, contradiction handling, uncertainty-aware revision, knowledge-base repair and semantic model update.

Explicitly distinguish adding another record from revising an existing belief/knowledge state.

## C. CONTEXTUALIZATION AND KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION
Investigate contextual learning, context-dependent policies, dynamic knowledge graphs, ontology evolution, relation learning, graph rewiring, abstraction/representation change and contextual specialization/generalization.

Distinguish:
new data/node
from
new relation
from
topological/representational reorganization
from
functional downstream consequence.

## D. CAUSAL LEARNING
Investigate causal discovery, causal representation learning, interventions, counterfactuals, active causal learning and experimental-design effects.

Prioritize intervention-based evidence that discriminates causal learning from correlation/pattern matching.

## E. METACOGNITION AND SELF-MODELING
Investigate confidence and uncertainty calibration, error/prediction monitoring, metacognitive sensitivity, selective prediction, strategy selection based on self-estimated competence, operational self-models and downstream behavioral use of self-monitoring.

Do not treat verbal self-description as sufficient evidence of metacognition.

## F. SELF-DIRECTED SCIENTIFIC EXPERIMENTATION
Investigate systems that generate hypotheses, identify informative experiments, select experiments, execute/orchestrate them, interpret results, update beliefs/hypotheses and select subsequent experiments from prior evidence.

Distinguish:
automated pipeline
from
closed-loop adaptive experimentation
from
genuinely self-directed scientific search.

## G. MACHINE CONSCIOUSNESS INDICATORS
Only after A–F.

Investigate theory-derived and computationally testable indicators, theory-neutral/adversarial tests, integration/global availability, self-model-related indicators and false-positive risks.

Do not infer phenomenal consciousness from verbal fluency, self-report, persistence or tool competence.

Treat "superconsciousness" only as an operational/falsifiable hypothesis, not an established property.

# 6. DELTA DISCRIMINATION

Use:

ΔW = parameter/weight change  
ΔM = memory/storage change  
ΔK = semantic knowledge/belief change  
ΔR = relation/topology/organization change  
ΔC = context/conditioning change  
ΔD = future decision/action-selection change  
ΔB = behavior change  
ΔO = downstream outcome change

Explicitly test the separations:

ΔM alone ≠ learning  
ΔW alone ≠ semantic knowledge revision  
ΔK alone ≠ relation reorganization  
ΔR alone ≠ future decision influence  
ΔD alone ≠ causal improvement  
ΔB alone ≠ understanding  
verbal self-report ≠ metacognitive control  
automated experiment execution ≠ self-directed experimentation.

For every major scientific claim, state which deltas are actually measured and which are merely inferred.

# 7. FALSE-POSITIVE / CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Explicitly search for controls against:
- memorization/retrieval mistaken for learning;
- prompt/context leakage;
- benchmark contamination;
- training/evaluation leakage;
- parameter fitting mistaken for semantic learning;
- repeated exposure mistaken for generalization;
- score changes without downstream usefulness;
- graph growth mistaken for meaningful relation reorganization;
- correlation mistaken for causal learning;
- verbal confidence mistaken for metacognition;
- scripted tool use mistaken for self-directed experimentation;
- human-designed experiment selection mistaken for autonomous scientific reasoning;
- persistence mistaken for future causal influence.

Prefer held-out evaluation, ablation, intervention, counterfactual, longitudinal, transfer/generalization and independent-replication designs where available.
