## OBJECTIVE

The conversation began with a practical runtime problem: IABV needed to prove that a human order was attached to the real foreground environment and flowed through one coherent chain from intake to decision, execution, verification and learning. As the work progressed, the objective broadened from order-flow correctness to a larger epistemic problem:

```text
Can IABV observe what is really on the machine,
separate raw evidence from interpretation,
show what it does not understand,
act only on grounded context,
retain experience,
reuse that experience on new cases,
and make scientifically defensible claims about learning?
```

The user repeatedly rejected purely declarative success. The standard became real runtime evidence, exact IDs, screenshots, audit bundles, explicit unknowns, causal caution, and source/data cross-checking.

---

## OBJECTIVE_EVOLUTION

1. **Canonical order flow** — one human order must produce one task/correlation/evidence chain and one decision/execution/verification/learning lifecycle.
2. **Canonical surface grounding** — the order must be attached to the real Windows foreground surface rather than an empty/default/simulated surface.
3. **Universal Cognitive State (UCS)** — maintain a unified cognitive snapshot containing surface, task, evidence, process, uncertainty, possible actions and operational memory.
4. **Live audit** — expose state transitions as snapshots so a human can audit raw perception, interpretation, decision, verification and cause/effect IDs.
5. **Visual perception calibration** — show the exact RAW image, an annotated “what IABV thinks it sees” view, and an unknown/gap view.
6. **Gap discovery** — unknowns must correspond to real visual/semantic uncertainty, not merely to a manifest saying a library is missing.
7. **Memory/generalization** — persistence is insufficient; prior experience should improve behavior on new wording/tasks without unsafe overgeneralization.
8. **Scientific boundary of learning** — classify components using code, runtime data and literature; distinguish self-adaptation, online learning, RL, continual learning, metacognition and heuristics.
9. **Historical preservation** — ensure the complete experience survives deletion of the original chat.
